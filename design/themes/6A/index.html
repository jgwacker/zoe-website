A Note from the Mapmaker
From the About page
Hi! I’m Zoe, a middle schooler who’s always juggling a bunch of different interests. I started this site to keep track of the things I’m working on, the ideas I’m excited about, and the projects that don’t always fit neatly into a school folder. Right now, I’m into geography and travel, playing cello and music, and parkour with mixed-martial arts. I also love discovering new places, digging into history, and snapping photos that tell little stories. Basically, it’s my highlight reel of experiments, adventures, and ‘aha!’ moments.
Zoe Menlo Park, California
